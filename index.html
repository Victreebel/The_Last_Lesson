The Last Lesson is a real-time strategy game. Choose a Campaign Theatre and Rival Doctrine to begin. Campaign Theatre shows the deterministic opening seed; press N to reroll it before a reign. Use arrow keys, a clear map edge, or middle-mouse drag to pan; B, D, H, R, and L open the Build, Accord, Heir, Realm, and Book panels; O opens the compact Orders drawer; Z toggles Field view, which clears the remaining command interface; M, A, and F choose move, attack, and advance orders; V orders selected battalions to hold their ground until the Crown gives a new order; selected battalions form a line around group movement and advance orders; Shift-right-click terrain queues additional movement waypoints for selected battalions; X toggles high-contrast presentation; Escape cancels the current command; Space pauses the local reign. The Full control requests browser fullscreen on wide tactical displays. While Build is open, its visible keys one through zero and Q, W, E select structures. While Heir is open, one rewards and two punishes the latest lesson. While Accord is open, one proposes the displayed prisoner exchange. While the Book is open, keys zero through nine activate its visibly numbered save, replay, export, presentation, and local-data actions.
